# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Giving and accepting feedback gracefully; assuming good intent.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code
of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/patbaumgartner), or by opening a private report if
GitHub's reporting tools are enabled for this repository. Reports are handled promptly and
confidentially.
